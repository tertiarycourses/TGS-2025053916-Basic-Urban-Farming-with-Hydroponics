# Activity 02: Commission, Operate and Restore a Hydroponics Kit

**Course:** Basic Urban Farming with Hydroponics (TGS-2025053916)  
**Version:** 2.0 · 29 August 2026

Prepare a kit, verify flow, establish a measured nutrient solution, transplant a seedling and return equipment to a clean serviceable state.

## Safety and scope

- [ ] Correct PPE available
- [ ] Chemical label and SDS available
- [ ] Damaged items quarantined
- [ ] Water and power separated

## Detailed activity sequence

1. **Read the SOP and identify every component and isolation point.**
   - Why: Correct identification prevents misassembly and unsafe intervention.
   - Evidence: Component and defect check complete.

2. **Inspect, assemble and leak-test before power-on.**
   - Why: Dry checks reduce water, stability and electrical hazards.
   - Evidence: Stable leak-free system; cables dry and protected.

3. **Power on and verify delivery, return and freeboard.**
   - Why: The farthest site and return establish functional flow.
   - Evidence: All sites wet; no overflow or abnormal operation.

4. **Measure source-water pH, EC and temperature.**
   - Why: A baseline is required before nutrient correction.
   - Evidence: Time, units, meter IDs and calibration status recorded.

5. **Mix two-part nutrient in the approved order.**
   - Why: Direct concentrate contact can precipitate nutrients.
   - Evidence: Dose and mixing times logged; no precipitate.

6. **Verify and incrementally correct EC and pH.**
   - Why: Small measured changes limit overshoot and chemical exposure.
   - Evidence: Before/action/after log within trainer setpoints.

7. **Accept and place one seedling.**
   - Why: Healthy uniform plugs protect crop consistency.
   - Evidence: Label, intact roots, centred support, stable crown.

8. **Perform the controlled power-off/restart test.**
   - Why: Commissioning must reveal overflow, siphon and restart risks.
   - Evidence: Safe drain-down and restored endpoint flow.

9. **Record final readings and obtain trainer verification.**
   - Why: The output state must be objectively verified.
   - Evidence: Completed final record and trainer sign-off.

10. **Isolate, drain, clean, rinse, dry, inspect and store.**
   - Why: Housekeeping and maintenance prevent contamination and preserve readiness.
   - Evidence: Dry complete kit and defect/maintenance record.
