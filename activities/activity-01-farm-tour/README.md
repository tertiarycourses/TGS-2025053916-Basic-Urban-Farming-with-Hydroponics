# Activity 01: MEOD Farm Observation and Control Checklist

**Course:** Basic Urban Farming with Hydroponics (TGS-2025053916)  
**Version:** 2.0 · 29 August 2026

Trace a live farm system, distinguish observed evidence from claims and identify operating, WSH, food-safety and maintenance controls.

## Safety and scope

- [ ] Emergency and movement rules understood
- [ ] Photography permission confirmed
- [ ] No equipment touched without permission
- [ ] Urgent hazards reported to trainer

## Detailed activity sequence

1. **Attend the site briefing and record permission boundaries.**
   - Why: Live farm operations and commercial information require controlled access.
   - Evidence: Briefing and photography permission boxes completed.

2. **Trace and sketch one complete water path.**
   - Why: The flow path reveals system boundary, sample/dose points and failure exposure.
   - Evidence: Labelled reservoir-to-return sketch.

3. **Compare crop condition across at least three positions.**
   - Why: Spatial patterns help diagnose flow, light and shared-input effects.
   - Evidence: Three factual notes using location/time.

4. **Record pH/EC/temperature/flow control evidence.**
   - Why: A control needs setpoint, measurement, response and verification.
   - Evidence: Instrument/frequency/action table completed.

5. **Inspect WSH, food safety, IPM, housekeeping and maintenance evidence.**
   - Why: Production compliance spans workers, produce and equipment.
   - Evidence: One existing control and one improvement opportunity.

6. **Peer-review and submit the evidence set.**
   - Why: Review removes vague inference and prohibited information.
   - Evidence: Signed PDF with only permitted images.
