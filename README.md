## About the course

This v2.1 release is a mechanism-led introduction to hydroponic urban farming. It covers root-zone control, system selection, water and nutrient management, crop health, current Singapore production cases, a guided farm tour, hydroponics-kit practice and aquaponics theory. The practical section now uses authentic local class and kit photographs from the Tertiary Infotech blog archive.

## Learning outcomes

Learners will be able to:

- perform farming activities and equipment operations according to standard operating procedures;
- execute farming operations in accordance with regulations, workplace safety and health practices, and housekeeping rules;
- observe and record commercial hydroponics-farm controls; and
- commission, measure, correct and return a basic hydroponics kit to a controlled state.

## Topics covered

- Hydroponics foundations, system types and crop selection
- Growing media, plant nutrition, pH and electrical conductivity
- Indoor-farm light, temperature, humidity and water controls
- Singapore hydroponics cases and urban-farming practices
- Farm-tour observation and evidence capture
- Hydroponics-kit commissioning, correction and cleaning
- Aquaponics principles and coupled-system risks
